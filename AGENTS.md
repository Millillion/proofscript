## Goal

Do not stop until you have created a language called `ProofScript` that produces formal verification proofs automatically from its own source code. `ProofScript` should essentially just be `TypeScript` but with formal verification annotations. If you write correct `ProofScript`, then you are able to produce a formal verification of the source code. There should be no other languages, syntaxes, tools, etc outside of `ProofScript` itself required to produce the formal verification proofs. You should be able to just write `ProofScript` and then know for certain that your behavior is correct under all circumstances.

You will know that you have successfully finished creating the compiler when you have written https://github.com/beatgammit/base64-js in `ProofScript` and have a formal verification proof of its behavior.
